Memoway · iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Overview

Memoway (“the App”) is a personal life-planning and reflection application for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Who we are

Memoway is provided by Julia Zhuravleva.
Contact: juliaezhik09@gmail.com
Website: juliaezhik.github.io/julia-zhuravleva-portfolio

Core principle

The iOS App is designed to keep your personal content on your device. The App does not require a Memoway cloud account and does not upload your journal, goals, habits, memories, job applications, German study data, or similar personal content to a Memoway server.

Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, it may store locally:

  • Profile details you enter (such as display name and optional email label)
  • Goals, tasks, memories, journal entries, mood logs, habits, calendar notes
  • Coach conversation history
  • Optional Job Applications and German learning content
  • App preferences (including which optional modules are enabled)
  • Locally scheduled reminder settings

This information remains on your device unless you back up, sync, or transfer the device using your own Apple/iCloud or computer backup tools.

Notifications

If you enable habit reminders and allow notifications, the App schedules local notifications on your device (including lock-screen alerts). These reminders are not sent through a Memoway push server.

Artificial intelligence features

On supported devices with Apple Intelligence available, the AI Coach may use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models to generate replies. In that case, coaching prompts and context are processed according to Apple’s on-device AI design.

If Apple Intelligence is unavailable, non-AI features continue to work offline; Coach replies may be unavailable until a supported model is available.

Optional modules

Job Applications and German are optional. They stay off until you enable them in Settings. Turning a module off hides it from your home screen; your previously saved local data for that module may remain on device until you delete it or remove the App.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

The iOS App does not send your personal Memoway content to third-party analytics or advertising networks.

Device permissions

The App may ask for:

  • Notifications — to deliver habit reminders you configure

You can change permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Data retention and deletion

Because data is stored on your device, deleting the App generally deletes the App’s local data (unless restored from a backup you control). You can also remove individual entries inside the App.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the App if you are under that age.

International users

Your content remains on your device. Any device backup/sync behavior follows your Apple account and device settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
